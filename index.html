Questions

Everything you might
want to know

Is the first call really free, no card needed? +

Yes, completely. You only need a phone number — no account, no credit card. You get a 5-minute call with your custom companion. After that, if you want more, you choose a plan.

How does the AI call my phone number? +

We use a telephony API to place a real outbound call to your number. The AI speaks in real-time using advanced voice synthesis — there's no robotic delay, it listens and responds naturally in conversation.

Does my companion remember our conversations? +

On paid plans, yes — your companion builds a memory of your conversations, learns your preferences, develops inside jokes, and builds relationship history over time. Free calls don't retain memory between sessions.

What languages are supported? +

Currently English and Hindi are fully supported. Support for Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, and Marathi is coming soon. The companion adapts to whichever language you prefer to speak in.

Is this safe and private? +

All conversations are encrypted and private to you. We never share or sell your data. You can delete your companion and all associated memory at any time from your account settings.
